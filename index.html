Beauty, in your hands.

The name joins two words: zuri, Swahili for beautiful, and luz, Spanish for light. Both ideas shape every bottle we make.

We develop each shade in small batches and test it for chip resistance and gloss before it ships. The result is polish that looks like a salon finish and feels like care.
